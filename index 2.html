Operating record

From fragmented rails to one dependable platform.

My role: Chief Product Officer; acting CTO during the 2024 regulatory tightening. I owned product strategy, platform roadmap, partner integrations and the operating model.

01 / Challenge

One merchant. Too many rails.

Cards, wallets, bank transfers, DCB and cross-border payouts were fragmented by market, partner and operating rule.

02 / Decisions

Make complexity a platform concern.

A stable API, canonical ledger, corridor-aware routing and shared risk controls turned local rails into one product surface.

03 / Outcomes

Scale without losing control.

$1B+ annual GTV, 270M+ payments a year and a 99.95% settlement SLA across 5 live markets.
