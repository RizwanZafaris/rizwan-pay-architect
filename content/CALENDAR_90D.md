## Distribution per slot

For every essay, ship in parallel:

1. LinkedIn post (teaser + image diagram + link)
2. X/Twitter thread (5–7 posts pulling key arguments)
3. Newsletter blast if list exists
4. Update `/blog` index, sitemap, RSS

## After day 90

Continue at 2/week from Batches 2–4. Re-prioritize based on:

- Search Console impressions per article
- LinkedIn engagement
- Recruiter inbound that cites specific essays
