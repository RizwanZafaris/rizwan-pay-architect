# {{Title}}

Use this when the searcher needs to understand how a payment, fintech or program system actually works.

## The Short Answer

Two direct paragraphs that can be quoted by AI search. Define the system, where it sits, and why it matters.

## Where It Fits In The Stack

- Customer / merchant surface
- API or operational layer
- Ledger / settlement / risk layer
- Partner, bank, wallet or scheme layer
- Compliance and evidence layer

## Reference Architecture

Explain the components, state changes, messages, failure points and ownership boundaries.

## Product Decisions That Matter

Name the choices a senior PM or program leader must make. Avoid generic advice.

## Failure Modes

Explain what breaks in production, how teams detect it, and what operating ritual prevents recurrence.

## Metrics

List success, risk, cost and reliability metrics.

## Rizwan's Operator Lens
