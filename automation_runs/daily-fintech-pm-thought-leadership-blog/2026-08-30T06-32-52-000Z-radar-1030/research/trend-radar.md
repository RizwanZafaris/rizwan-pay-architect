## 1. SAMA open-banking licensing operating model

Label: timely-current
Disposition: website article created and passed local gates; use as today's single website candidate.
Candidate organizations: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA); Tatbiq Darahem Company for Technology Almaliya; Malaa Company for Information Technology.

Why now:
- SAMA announced on 2026-03-26 that it had commenced licensing fintech companies to provide open banking services after the regulatory sandbox phase.
- SAMA licensed Tatbiq Darahem for payment services by providing account information on 2026-05-10.
- SAMA licensed Malaa to provide payment account information services on 2026-07-22 and said there were then 33 licensed payment-service companies in Saudi Arabia.
- SAMA's open-banking framework material and rulebook articles make this a product operating model across consent, access, authentication, denial handling, data minimization, payment initiation boundaries and recovery.
