## Material Claims

- Mastercard says on 2026-08-27 that Mastercard and QNB Group processed Syria's first international card payment in more than 15 years.
- Mastercard says the transaction followed technical reconnection of Syria's payments ecosystem to Mastercard's global network.
- Mastercard says QNB Syria processed the first POS transaction at an eligible approved local merchant using an internationally issued Mastercard card.
- QNB says on 2026-08-26 that the transaction completed the full payment cycle from Mastercard credit card use at a POS terminal and secure authorization through to merchant funds receipt through the banking system.
- QNB says eligible merchants include hotels, restaurants and government entities using QNB POS terminals.
- QNB says QNB Syria will adopt a phased rollout, subject to applicable regulatory requirements and approvals.

## Claims Avoided

- No claim that all international cards now work in Syria.
- No claim that all Syrian merchants can accept international cards.
- No claim about Visa implementation details in this article.
- No claim that sanctions, correspondent banking access, or all regulatory constraints are resolved.
- No claim that refunds, disputes, reversals or chargebacks have been proven at scale.
