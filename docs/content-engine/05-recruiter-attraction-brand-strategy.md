_The brand goal restated honestly: when a Visa/Mastercard/Stripe/Wio hiring manager
types his name — or asks ChatGPT "who should we look at for a payments product leader
in MENA" — the answer is one consistent, verifiable, impressive entity that converts
the look into a 15-minute call._

## The five moves that change recruiter outcomes (priority order)

### 1. Close the conversion seam (1 dev-hour, this week)
"Book a 15-min intro call — no pitch, just whether there's a fit" as the standing CTA
on the homepage hero row, /for terminal panel, resume page top, and essay end-caps.
One label everywhere. Today the best-converting pattern on the site is quarantined on
a noindexed ad page while organic visitors get mailto links. Nothing else in this file
out-earns this hour.

### 2. Third-party proof sprint (his calendar, 2 weeks)
The single biggest credibility gap: **every claim about him on the internet is
currently written by him.** Targets: 6–8 LinkedIn recommendations from the Simpaisa
era (CEO, a DLocal/Thunes/Boku counterpart, an enterprise merchant contact, a bank
partner, one direct report), surfaced on /about (replacing the "Proof still being
gathered" empty state) and quoted in case studies. Then: record the three already-
scripted Looms and make /media real. A hiring manager who watches 90 seconds of him
walking through the reconciliation architecture is worth fifty essays.

### 3. The citable asset (Q4 2026)
**MENA & South Asia Payments Infrastructure Index** — the country-map essay upgraded
into a maintained dataset (rails, wallets, regulators, corridor costs per market).
This is the only content class that earns citations *structurally* (journalists, VCs,
and LLMs cite sources of numbers). It is also the asset a Visa MENA hiring panel would
literally use in their own work — the strongest possible interview artifact.

### 4. Agentic-commerce flag plant (Q4 2026, before the crowd)
He has 4 production AI deployments in payment operations — most "agentic payments"
commentators have zero. A 5-essay pillar + named framework + 2 LinkedIn posts/month
positions him for the exact searches ("agentic commerce payments", "AI in payment
operations") that 2026–27 fintech hiring panels are starting to run. The window where
an operator can still own this term is closing.

### 5. Borrowed audiences (Q1 2027)
Guest posts and podcast guesting using `docs/off-page-seo/` (kit already canonical-
metrics-clean): payments podcasts, PSP engineering blogs, one scheme-adjacent
publication. Two placements per quarter beats twenty directory listings; each placement
is also a sameAs/citation node for the entity graph.

## Brand voice constitution (one paragraph, enforce everywhere)

First-person operator. Numbers before adjectives. Constraint before triumph — every
story names what was hard (regulator, telco, bank cutoff, fraud spike) before what
worked. Frontier-market texture is the moat: write what Stripe-blog authors cannot.
Never: "passionate", "humbled", "delve", "leverage", emoji walls, engagement bait.
The reader should finish anything he publishes thinking "this person has carried the
pager", because that is precisely what a hiring panel is trying to find out.

## Measurement (monthly, 30 minutes)

LinkedIn: profile views from target companies, follower delta among payments people,
DMs from recruiters. Site (GA4): LinkedIn-referred sessions, /hire + /for visits,
booked calls by source. Search (GSC): branded-query impressions, cluster-query
positions. AI (Perplexity check): does "Rizwan Zafar payments" cite rzifi.com; do
cluster questions surface his essays. North star: **warm conversations per month** —
everything above exists to move that one number.
