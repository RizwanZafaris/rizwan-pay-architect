## Post 1: Payment Infrastructure

Payment infrastructure is not finished when the API returns success.

It is finished when:

- finance can reconcile it
- risk can explain it
- compliance can audit it
- operations can recover it
- the merchant can trust it
- the customer knows where the money is

That is the part of payments product management people under-discuss.

At $1B+ GTV scale, the product is not just checkout, routing or rails. It is the operating model around money state.

## Post 2: AI In Payments

AI in payments should start where the risk is bounded.

Good early use cases:

- merchant integration support
- incident triage
- partner support automation
- KYB document extraction
- risk-review assist
- reconciliation exception explanation

Bad first use case:

- autonomous money-movement decisions with no audit model

In regulated payments, AI has to improve the workflow before it earns the right to influence the decision.

## Post 3: Payments PM

Payments product managers cannot prioritize only with value vs effort.

They also need risk-cost:

- What happens if settlement breaks?
- What happens if fraud rises?
- What happens if the scheme rejects the flow?
- What happens if the regulator asks for the audit trail?
- What happens if the merchant cannot reconcile?

That third dimension changes the roadmap.
