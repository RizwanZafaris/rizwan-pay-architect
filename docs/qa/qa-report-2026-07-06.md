## 1. Findings fixed in this round (all verified in the built output)

### [P0] `/journey/` shipped literal `TODO(owner)` scaffold text to visitors — FIXED (`4705610`)
The Nigeria market card rendered three internal strings on the live page: `TODO(OWNER)` in the years slot, `TODO(OWNER)` as the brand line, and *"TODO(owner): confirm a concrete Nigeria shipped item — no supporting bullet in profile.experience yet."* as body copy. Found by the independent reviewer; on a page whose entire purpose is proving operating discipline to people doing diligence, leaked scaffold language falsifies the positioning.
**Fix:** neutral visitor copy ("In verification" / "Corridor work" / "Engagement detail is being verified before publication…"), the owner TODO moved to a source comment, `needsOwnerConfirm` still excludes Nigeria from all structured data. **Plus a new build gate:** `seo-audit.ts` now fails on `/TODO\s*\(/` in any emitted HTML — this class of bug cannot ship again.

### [P0-by-gate] `/speaking/` also shipped `TODO(owner): headshot download URL` — FIXED (`4705610`)
Caught by the new gate on its first run (both human reviews missed it — the page is parked/noindex, but it's reachable by direct link). Replaced with a real "available on request → contact page" line.

### [P1] Desktop `/journey/` timeline rail sliced through body text — FIXED (`4705610`)
The 2px rail sat at `left:50%` while era content was a *centered* 46% column spanning it — the rail ran through every paragraph (a leftover broken `nth-child` arbitrary variant showed the alternating design was started but never finished). Implemented the alternating layout properly: odd eras right of the rail, even eras left, both fully clear. **Measured proof at 1280px: rail x=639, era text 674–1064, `railSlicesText: false`.**

### [P1] World-map labels at 375px were ~8.5px (illegible) and cluster association was ambiguous — FIXED (`eebf044`)
Task 2 of this round: two build-time collision layouts (desktop 1.35px / mobile 2.2px with a wider candidate ring) toggled by media query; **leader lines** from pin to any displaced label (the Egypt/UAE/KSA/Pakistan cluster was exactly the judgment case — verdict: needed); paper text halo (`paint-order: stroke`) for legibility over dots and arcs; "United Arab Emirates"→"UAE" on the map only. Verified by screenshot at both widths. Still zero client JS.

### [P1] "since 2009" vs "Seventeen years" self-contradiction — FIXED (`eebf044`)
Task 1: duration framing everywhere (hero + meta, proof band, about-band sticker, /about title, both /speaking CFP bios, /journey meta/OG). The count is **computed at build time** from `CAREER_START_YEAR` in `profile.ts` — the site rebuilds daily, so "17 years" rolls to 18 automatically in January 2027 and can never go stale. The two-tier gate's career-marker regex was widened (`1[7-9]|2\d years`) so it keeps protecting after the rollover — a real gap, since the literal `/17 years/` would have silently lapsed.
