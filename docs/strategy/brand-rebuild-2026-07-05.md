## 1. Brand audit — why the current site under-positions you

The site is technically excellent (five audits this month: 163 pages healthy, CLS 0.00, CI fact-gates, top-decile a11y). The brand problem is **scope compression**:

1. **The site sells one job, not a 17-year arc.** Everything above the fold is Simpaisa-scoped (5 markets, $1B+ GTV). Your career scope — **operating since 2009, ten markets (Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Iraq, Egypt, KSA, UAE, Nigeria, Sri Lanka, Myanmar), three industries (e-commerce, OTT, payments)** — appears nowhere as a first-class claim. Daraz and Tapmad exist only as resume lines and two case studies.
2. **Positioned as a candidate, not a category.** "Open to senior roles" is the loudest identity signal. Executives/founders/investors don't hire "open to roles" — they engage "the person who owns frontier-market payments."
3. **Proof is asserted, not witnessed.** Zero testimonials; the merchant marquee is platform-scope proof without labeling; media/speaking page is all `comingSoon`; credentials (MIT Sloan, PMI Youngest PM of the Year 2015, PMI Karachi VP) are buried on /resume.
4. **The audience model is recruiter-only.** Nav says "For recruiters"; the contact form asks what role you're hiring for; founders/investors/partners have no doorway (advisory parked until Oct 2 — fine — but the *brand* can still speak to them).
5. **No visual signature.** The paper-editorial system is distinctive and worth keeping, but there is no single memorable brand visual. For a 10-market operator, the obvious one is missing: **the map**.

## 7. Technical architecture (recommendation + migration gate)

**Recommendation: keep the TanStack-static chassis for the brand rebuild.** Reasons: (1) the Codex automation writes markdown → repo → CI — it IS your CMS, and Next/Supabase adds surface without solving a problem you have; (2) SEO momentum just started (indexing fixed Jun 12; best organic day Jul 2) — a replatform churns URLs/rendering at the worst moment; (3) the JS-less build is a genuine performance/a11y differentiator (CLS 0.00) that most Next sites never reach; (4) every requested capability (CMS/blog, schema, OG, sitemap, analytics, lead capture, newsletter) already exists and passed audit.
**Next.js migration gate (revisit ~Oct with the advisory re-launch):** migrate only if you need ≥2 of: authenticated areas, DB-backed personalization, on-demand ISR content, a team editing via UI. If triggered: Next 15 App Router + shadcn/ui + 21st.dev components (native there) + Supabase (auth/db) + Contentlayer-style MD pipeline reusing the same content files — the copy/IA/design system from THIS document ports 1:1.
**Schema upgrades now:** Person → add `award` (PMI 2015), `alumniOf` (already MIT Sloan), `workLocation` list, `worksFor` + past `Organization`s (Daraz, Tapmad), `knowsAbout` refresh; /journey gets an `ItemList` of market stops; /speaking gets `Event`/`PodcastEpisode` as they land.
