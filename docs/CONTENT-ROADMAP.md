# Content Roadmap — rzifi.com

The realistic 3-month content plan. Built from Rizwan's 30-topic idea dump + existing cluster gaps + recruiter-search keyword priorities.

**Headline number:** 30 new essays + 10 new case studies over ~12 weeks, taking the site from 55 → 85 essays and 10 → 20 case studies. Quality > quantity. No bulk AI generation.

---

## Why not 1500

The original ask was 1500 essays + 750 case studies. Strategically wrong for personal-brand SEO. The argument in short:

- **Quality compound.** 100 deep, well-targeted essays in 5 clusters beat 1500 thin essays in 50 clusters for both Google ranking (since the March 2024 Helpful Content Update) and recruiter perception
- **Voice integrity.** Bulk-generated content loses Rizwan's operator-grade voice. Recruiters notice within two paragraphs
- **Domain age.** rzifi.com is a new domain. Publishing 200+ essays/month on a new domain triggers Google's spam classifier
- **The math.** 1500 essays at the standard of the existing 55 = 6–8 years of full-time writing OR loses quality

The path that actually moves recruiter inbound and search rankings: **measured, deep, cluster-aligned essays** — 30 new pieces over 3 months, each filling a specific gap.

## Why not bulk AI-generate via OpenRouter

Two reasons.

1. **Voice integrity.** The existing 55 essays have a specific operator-grade voice — operator-confident, precise about scope, willing to name failures. AI-generated drafts at scale lose this within 5 essays. Mixing AI-generated with hand-written content makes the cluster feel inconsistent.

2. **Helpful Content risk.** Google's March 2024 update specifically targets sites that publish AI-generated content at volume. Detection is good and getting better. The downside risk for a new domain (rzifi.com is <6 months old as a canonical) is whole-site deprioritisation. Not worth it.

**Where AI generation makes sense:** first drafts of routine sub-sections (e.g., the "FAQ" section at the bottom of each essay), translation of an existing essay into a new language for international SEO, or summarising a long essay into a shorter LinkedIn post. Not the body work.

## How to add more essays after Week 12

Once the 30 essays are shipped:

- **Track GSC impressions per essay.** Some essays will get 10× the traffic of others. Write more in those clusters.
- **Track inbound recruiter searches.** If "MPGS product manager" or "MDES tokenisation lead" is showing up in GSC queries, write the next essay in that cluster.
- **Watch the AI citation rate.** If ChatGPT / Perplexity start citing your essays for specific queries, write more variations in those queries.

The 30-essay plan is the seed. After that, it's a measurement-driven content programme, not a target-driven one.
